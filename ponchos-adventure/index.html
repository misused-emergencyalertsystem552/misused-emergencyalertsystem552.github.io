How to play

Poncho the cat must cross the keep's rooms. In each room, collect every Heart Framer to open the chest, take the jewel, and leave through the door that opens at the top.

Push green Emerald Framers to block gazes. Gold-framed purple hearts give 2 magic shots: hit a monster to turn it into an egg, hit the egg to send it away for a while. Eggs can be pushed, and they float on water. Trees stop your feet but not shots or stares. Later rooms add grey hearts that give a Hammer for breaking one rock, teal hearts that give a Bridge for crossing one water or lava tile (press X), plus sand that slows you, grass that monsters cannot enter, and river currents that carry eggs.
